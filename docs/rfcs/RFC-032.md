# RFC 32: Water That Is a Brush Entity (the Pool of `ttt_67thway_rework_b1`)

> **Status: proposed (2026-11-15), not built.** Asked for by the maintainer after a round replay on `ttt_67thway_rework_b1` showed the pool at the diving board as a dry grey floor ("why no water?!"). Extends RFC 18 (fuller 3D maps: water, brush entities, 4.1 and 4.2); the replay page is RFC 12.

## 1. Abstract
The map extractor draws two kinds of water: liquid faces of the world model (RFC 18, 4.1) and liquid faces of the brush entities it is told to draw (4.2: `func_brush`, `func_wall`, `func_breakable`, `func_lod`, `func_wall_toggle`). A third kind is missed: **`func_water_analog`**, Source's own moving water level, a brush entity whose water surface the mapper placed at the pool. It is on neither list, so its faces are counted as "other model faces" and never drawn. On `ttt_67thway_rework_b1` the pool at the diving board is exactly this entity, so the replay and the map page show a dry floor where players swim.

This RFC adds the class, draws its water faces where the map puts the water at the start of the round, and adds the Water checkbox to the replay page (the map page has one; the replay does not).

## 2. What the map shows (measured 2026-11-15)
The map is Workshop item `577197148` (`ttt_67thway_rework_b1`, by iluka), downloaded anonymously with the pinned DepotDownloader (RFC 11, 3.2; the archive is LZMA-compressed, which the backend's own code already reads) and read with the project's extractor; the file is not kept in the repository.

*   **The world model has water.** The statistics say `waterFaces: 18`, which become **54 water triangles** (in the extractor's `water` list), in a box of x -2064 to 864, y -904 to 1828, z -2560 to 64: low-lying coast water, inside the box of the floors, so the "within 512 units of the floors and walls" rule of `mapgeometry.ts` keeps it. Nine materials carry `SURF_WARP` (`nature/water_coast01_beneath` and eight `maps/ttt_67thway_rework_b1/nature/water_coast01_<x>_<y>_<z>`).
*   **The pool is a brush entity.** The entity lump has one `func_water_analog` (`hammerid 173539`, `targetname "water"`, model `*37`) with `origin "-768 40 -528"`, `startposition 0`, `movedistance 140`, `movedir "-90 0 0"`, `speed 20`, `WaveHeight 3.0`, `startsound "water_flood_in"`, and a `water_lod_control`. Model 37 is a box of 256 x 368 x 144 units (mins -128, -184, -72, maxs 128, 184, 72, around its origin) with **two faces**: its surface (`maps/ttt_67thway_rework_b1/nature/water_coast01_-767_32_-399`) and its underside (`nature/water_coast01_beneath`), both flagged `SURF_WARP` (flags `0x3408`). The extractor's rule "only upward faces (normal z of 0.5 or more) are water" keeps the surface and drops the underside, which is right.
*   **Why it is missing.** `extractMapEntities` (`bsp.ts`) turns an entity into a drawn model only when its class matches `FIXTURE_CLASSES` or `MOVABLE_CLASSES`. `func_water_analog` matches neither, so its two faces fall into `otherModelFaces`. The pool in the screenshot is the floor beneath the water brush.
*   **What it means in the game.** In Source the `movedir` angles of `-90 0 0` point **up** (pitch -90 is up, +90 is down), `startposition 0` is "at the place the mapper put it", and the sound is a flood: the water is low at the start of the round and rises by `movedistance` (140 units) when the map triggers it (`speed 20` units a second). That reading is Source's convention as I know it; it is not verified on a running server (section 7).

## 3. Design
*   **One new class set**, next to the two existing ones in `bsp.ts`: `WATER_CLASSES = /^func_water_analog$/` (only this class; `func_water` does not exist in Source's games, and a `func_brush` with a water texture is already drawn by the fixture rule, because the `SURF_WARP` test applies to every drawn model's faces). The entity is read like a fixture (`extractMapEntities` returns its model number and its origin, with the angles it has) and marked `water: true`.
*   **Only its liquid faces are kept.** For a `water: true` fixture the face loop keeps the faces with `SURF_WARP` and an upward normal and drops every other face of that model (a `func_water_analog` is a water volume: its sides and bottom are not floors or walls and must not become a second storey, the failure of RFC 18, 4.1). They go to the `water` list, not to `floors` or `walls`, so the level model is not affected.
*   **Where.** The faces are stored around the model's own origin (RFC 18, 4.2, as for every fixture). They are placed at the entity's `origin`, then **moved along `movedir` by `startposition` x `movedistance`** (a number the map file gives: 0 here, so the water stays where the mapper put it). `movedir` is turned into a direction with the same `angleMatrix` the fixtures use. A missing or malformed value counts as 0. The result is the water at the **start** of the round.
*   **What is not drawn.** The rising of the water in a round (the map triggers it; the bridge sends no event for it) is not shown: the replay and the map page show the water at its starting level. Showing the flood would need an event from the game for the water entity (a position track like RFC 20's parts) and is not part of this RFC.
*   **Replay page.** The replay already calls `prepareWater` on the loaded model, but has no way to switch it. Add the **Water** checkbox that the map page has (`data-water`, on by default, shown only when the map has water: `geometry.info.water` is already in the replay's data or is added to it), in the controls row beside Walls and Leaves, in the page's existing style (RFC 30's spacing, no layout jump).
*   **Mesh version.** The extractor's output changes, so stored meshes must be rebuilt: `MESH_VERSION` 12 to 13 and `MESH_VERSION_GAME` 13 to 14, with the line in `meshVersion.ts`'s history ("water that is a brush entity", RFC 32). The dashboard rebuilds stored meshes in the background, as it did for every earlier change.

## 4. Limits, safety and cost
*   The entity count and face limits that already guard the extractor apply (`SurfaceLimits`); a map with many `func_water_analog` entities is bounded by the same face and vertex caps as everything else.
*   Cost: two faces on this map (the surface is a quad: 2 triangles). Nothing is added to the replay's per-frame work.
*   Nothing is trusted from the map beyond what the extractor already reads: `startposition`, `movedistance` and `movedir` are parsed as finite numbers, `startposition` is limited to 0 to 1 and `movedistance` to a sane bound (for example 0 to 16 384 units, `MAX_COORD` of `props.ts` is the model), anything else counts as 0.

## 5. Stages
*   **A (this RFC):** the class set, the face rule, the placement, the mesh version, the replay checkbox, tests, a measurement on the real map (the water-triangle count of the map before and after; a render of the replay from the same camera as the screenshot, showing the water), the docs (RFC 18, 4.2 gets a line pointing here).
*   **B (only if wanted):** the flood: a water level in the track, so the replay shows it rise; needs a bridge event and RFC 20's part tracking.

## 6. Tests (what must fail on a broken build)
*   `bsp` extractor on a small constructed BSP (the tests of RFC 18 build one): a `func_water_analog` with a two-face model (an upward `SURF_WARP` surface and a downward one) gives **one water quad at the entity's origin and nothing in `floors` or `walls`**; the same map without the class change gives none (proves the class set is the cause).
*   `startposition 0.5`, `movedistance 100`, `movedir "-90 0 0"` moves the water up 50 units; `movedir "90 0 0"` moves it down; malformed values (`"abc"`, `NaN`, `-1`, `2`, a huge distance) behave as 0.
*   A `func_water_analog` model with a non-liquid face does not add that face anywhere.
*   `func_brush` with a water texture is still drawn as water (no regression), `func_door` with a water texture is still not drawn.
*   The mesh version numbers differ by one and are bumped, and a stored mesh of the old version is rebuilt (the existing version tests).
*   The replay page shows the Water checkbox when the map has water and not otherwise; unchecking hides the water group; the row does not change size when toggled (the layout-jump and contrast tests); the checkbox follows RFC 31's toggle style if that is the page's pattern for checkboxes (it is the shared `.check` style now).
*   A test on the real map is not possible in CI (the file is not in the repository); the measurement of 5 is recorded in this RFC instead.

## 7. What cannot be verified here
*   Source's reading of `startposition` and `movedir` (section 2) is from the game's documented behaviour, not from a running server; the first replay after the build shows whether the water sits in the pool at the right height. If it sits too high or too low, the sign of the move is the thing to change.
*   Other maps: whether any of the server's other maps uses `func_water_analog` or another liquid brush entity class. Before building, run the extractor over the server's stored maps and list the classes of every non-world model that has a `SURF_WARP` face; a class that shows up there and is not drawn gets the same treatment (stage A should include that survey and its result in this RFC).
*   Whether the translucent water looks right over the pool's floor in the replay's three.js scene (RFC 18's water material is shared with the world water, which does look right on `ttt_minecraft_haven`).
