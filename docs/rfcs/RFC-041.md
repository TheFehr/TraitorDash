# RFC 41: Recording After the Round Ends

> **Status: proposed (2026-11-21), not built.** Asked for by the maintainer: "our old recordings stop abruptly as the round ends, we do newly record pre-round, can we record a configurable amount of seconds of post-round too?" Builds on RFC 12 (the track; 3.7, the preparation before the round), RFC 23 (the shared settings page) and RFC 26 (the events).

## 1. Abstract
A track begins before the round (the preparation, RFC 12, 3.7: `traitordash_track_prep`) but ends the moment the round ends, so the replay stops on the last second of play: the winning kill, a body still falling, the last shot. This RFC records a configurable number of seconds **after** the round ends, so that the replay shows what happens next (the reveal, the winners, the last moments), and marks where the round ended.

## 2. Rules
1.  **Configurable, off-able:** a per-server setting `traitordash_track_post`, a whole number of **seconds**, 0 to 60, default **10**; 0 records nothing after the end, as today. It is the **seventeenth** setting of the shared settings page (RFC 23), in "Position tracking", section "What else to record" beside the preparation switch, applies at the next round.
2. **The round's own end stays the round's end:** the statistics, the round's duration, the result and the deaths of the round are as before. What is recorded after the end is the track's, for the replay only. Nothing after the end is counted anywhere.
3. **Same recording, same limits:** the same positions, the same events and the same caps (RFC 12, 26), the same tick limit (`traitordash_track_max_ticks`): the post-round seconds use the room the round left, and stop when it is full. No new data kind.
4. **Stops early** when the next round's preparation begins, the map changes or the server shuts down, whichever comes first; the track is sent at that point with what it has. A player who leaves is simply not sampled any more.
5. **Old tracks and old bridges are unchanged:** a track without the end marker is a track that ends at its end.

## 3. Design
### 3.1 The bridge (`track.lua`)
*   At the round's end the recording is **not** finished and sent at once when the setting is above 0: it notes the end (`ended`, milliseconds since the round began, the same clock as the ticks), keeps its sampling timer running, and **finishes** when `post` seconds have passed, or at the early stops of rule 4. Everything else about finishing (the segments, the events, the size, the POST) is the existing code, run later.
*   Who is sampled: the players of the round, as during the round (rule 2's "same recording"). A player alive at the end keeps being sampled; one dead stays dead (their body). The roles are revealed at the end by the game, and the `role` events keep coming from the existing hook if the game fires them.
*   Events keep being recorded (chat, damage, pickups, the rest): after the end of a TTT round the game lets the survivors act (and chat) until the map or the next round, and it is what makes the end readable. The per-kind caps hold.
*   The state is per round and survives only until the finish; a reload of the file or a crash loses the post-round part, never the round (the round's track is sent at the end of the post-round time; if the bridge stops before that, nothing is sent for the round: see section 6, open).

### 3.2 The track and the backend
*   One new optional top-level number, `ended`: the round's end in ms since the round began, an integer from 0 to the last tick's time. `validateTrack` checks it (and drops it when absent); a track with `ended` equal to its last tick, or without it, is as before. The ticks, deaths and events after `ended` are checked as any others.
*   `begin` (the preparation) and `ended` together say the three parts of a track: before the round, the round, after it. The storage, the limits and the pruning (RFC 12, 3.4) are unchanged: the post-round seconds are counted in the track's size like any others (at 5 samples a second, 10 seconds is 50 samples a player: **about 1 to 2 %** more for a round of 3 to 4 minutes; to be measured on the first real round).

### 3.3 The replay
*   The time bar covers the whole track as it already does (it already starts before 0 for the preparation); a **marker at the end of the round** (a tick like the death ticks, labelled "Round end") and the time display "3:28 / 3:58" as now, the total including the post-round part.
*   After the end the replay behaves as before for everything (players, bodies, events, the feed); a player who is dead at the end is a body as before. The result line of the page still says what the round's result was. A track with no `ended` has no marker.
*   The page's "Selected player" panel and the totals (RFC 26) count what is in the track, so damage dealt after the end would show in a player's totals: **proposed: only up to the round's end** (the totals are of the round), the feed shows everything.

## 4. What this does not do
*   No change to the statistics or to what counts as the round.
*   No recording between rounds beyond the preparation and this: the time after the post-round seconds until the next preparation is not recorded.
*   No separate setting for what is recorded after the end: it is the same as in the round.

## 5. Tests (what must fail on a broken build)
*   **Bridge** (the Fengari harness): with `post` 0 the track is sent at the end as before, with no `ended`; with `post` 10 it is not sent at the end, samples keep coming for 10 s of the fake clock and it is sent after them with `ended` at the round's end; the early stops (the next preparation, the map change, the shutdown) send it at once; a round that has no time left under the tick limit stops sampling and sends; the setting is clamped to 0..60; events after the end are recorded and capped; nothing changes for a recording that was not on.
*   **Backend:** `ended` accepted inside the ticks' range and refused outside it or when fractional, absent is fine; a track with ticks after `ended` is valid; the stored track round-trips it.
*   **Replay:** the marker at `ended`, none without; the total time includes the post-round part; the totals of a player stop at `ended`; a track from before is unchanged.
*   **Settings:** the setting appears on the shared page in its section with its range, is sent to the bridge like the others and applies at the next round (RFC 23's tests of the list).
*   **Mutation checks** on: the early stops, the clamp, the totals' cut at `ended`, the marker.

## 6. Open
*   **The default** (10 s proposed; the game's own post-round time is 30 s by default, so 30 would show all of it).
*   **A bridge that stops during the post-round time** loses that round's track (proposed: it is acceptable, it is rare) or the track is sent at the end as before and the post-round part is a second upload that extends it (more parts, more risk): proposed the first.
*   **The totals' cut** (3.3): proposed.
*   **Verification on the real server** (required before it is called done): play a round with the setting at 10 and watch the end: the marker, the survivors, the reveal; and check the size of the track against one without.
