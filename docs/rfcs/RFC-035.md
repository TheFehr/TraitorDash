# RFC 35: Doors and Lifts at Their Round-Start Position (the Pool Cover of `ttt_community_pool`)

> **Status: built (2026-11-17).** See section 10 for what differs from the design. Asked for by the maintainer after the replay of `ttt_community_pool` showed no pool water: the water is drawn, but under a slab. Extends RFC 33 (the missing map elements) and RFC 18 (4.2 and 4.4: brush entities and parts that move, drawn "at their starting place"). Found by reading the map (2026-11-16, Workshop item 118623480).

## 1. Abstract
RFC 18, 4.4 draws the doors, lifts and other parts that can move "where the map file puts them, a door shut". For many parts that is the **wrong** place: the map file stores the part at its *origin* (its closed position), and a part that the map **starts open** is moved to its open end when the round begins. The extractor never reads that start state, so it draws such a part in its closed position, which is the trap's **active** state, not what a player sees at round start.

On `ttt_community_pool` the pool is covered by a `func_door` named "pool cover" with `spawnpos 1` (starts open; a traitor button closes it to drown the people in the pool). We draw it closed, a slab at z 40 to 66 lying on the water plane at z 41, so the pool shows no water. The same pattern is on `ttt_67thway_rework_b1` (`trapdoor1`, `trapdoor2`, `scandoor`, all `spawnpos 1`) and on the bathroom doors of `community_pool` itself.

This RFC reads each moving part's **start state** and draws the part there: at its open end, or not at all where the open end cannot be placed with confidence.

## 2. What the maps show (measured 2026-11-16)
*   **`ttt_community_pool`.** The water is 4 upward `SURF_WARP` faces at z 41 (material `liquids/water_pretty1`), x -697 to 505, y 1353 to 2231: drawn correctly, 54 water triangles in the built mesh, the Water checkbox present. The cover is `func_door` model `*52`, origin `-96 1791.5 53`, box x -697 to 505, y 1352 to 2231, z 40 to 66, 22 faces, `spawnpos 1`, `spawnflags 0`, `movedir 0 0 0` (east: Source's `movedir` of pitch 0 yaw 0 points along +x), `lip -8`, `speed 120`, `forceclosed 1`. Its connections: the traitor button "pool cover button" sends `Close`, the button "saveme" sends `Open`, and the door's own `OnFullyClosed` sends `Open` after 30 s. Our extractor draws it at its origin as the movable part `movable-52` (50 triangles). Open, it slides along +x by about 1 210 units (the 1 202 wide slab plus the lip) to x 513 to 1715, away from the pool. The pool basin is deep (the bottom at about z -277), so with the cover gone the water plane is a plain pool.
*   **The bathroom doors of the same map** ("door 1", "door 2": `*45`, `*47`, `spawnpos 1`, `movedir 90 0 0`, i.e. DOWN) belong to the flood trap: the button closes them, the water trap's `OnFullyClosed` opens them. We draw them closed; at round start they are open (sunk into the floor), so the doorway is free. `testerdoor` (`func_door`) and `t stall` (`func_door_rotating`) are `spawnpos 1` too.
*   **`ttt_67thway_rework_b1`:** `trapdoor1`, `trapdoor2` (closed by the traitor "emergency" button and a `func_button`) and `scandoor`: `spawnpos 1`; drawn closed, wrong. Two `func_tracktrain`s start by `logic_auto` `OnMapSpawn StartForward`: that affects motion only, not the pose we draw.
*   **`ttt_minecraft_b7_summer`:** no `func_door` with `spawnpos 1`, no `startposition`; its on/off brush pairs use `StartDisabled`, which RFC 33 already reads.
*   **The other 25 maps are not read yet.** Expect the pattern wherever a trap is built from a door; the census of section 5 measures it.

## 3. How Source says a part starts (what is read)
*   **`func_door`:** `spawnpos` (0: starts closed, the default; 1: starts open) and the `spawnflags` bit 1 ("starts open", older maps), `movedir` (pitch yaw roll; the direction of travel when opening; pitch -90 is up, 90 is down), `lip` (units left inside the frame at the open end; may be negative), `speed`. The open end is the origin moved along `movedir` by `|movedir · size| - lip`, where `size` is the model's box (the part travels exactly its own extent along that direction, less the lip).
*   **`func_door_rotating`:** `spawnpos`/`spawnflags` bit 1, `distance` (degrees to turn, 90 by default), the axis from `spawnflags` (bit 64: X, bit 128: Y, else Z), the direction reversed by bit 2, and the `angles` of the entity; the open end is the part turned about its origin by `distance` around that axis.
*   **`func_movelinear`:** `startposition` (0 to 1, the fraction of `movedistance` along `movedir` at the start; default 0).
*   Parts that start moving by themselves (`func_tracktrain` by `logic_auto`, `func_rotating`) are outside this RFC: their pose at the start is their origin, as now.
*   **Not read: the map's own I/O at the start.** A part that the map toggles when the round starts through an entity connection (e.g. `t2_blocker` of `b7_summer`, switched off by a role-assignment connection, `ttt_logic_role OnPass`) is still drawn as the map file stores it. Reading connections is a separate, larger task and is not part of this RFC (section 7).

## 4. Design
*   **Where.** `extractMapEntities` (`bsp.ts`) already builds a `Fixture` (model, origin, angles, `movable`, class, name) for each brush entity of `MOVABLE_CLASSES`; the start state changes the **origin** (and for a rotating door the angles) it records. A new pure function `startPose(entity, model box)` returns the pose or `null`; it takes the entity's keyvalues (read in lower case) and the model's mins and maxs (the models lump, as the water brush's placement already uses).
*   **`func_door`:** if the part starts open, the pose is the origin plus `dir * (|dir · size| - lip)`, `dir` from `movedir` through the same `angleMatrix` the fixtures use (applied to (1, 0, 0), the forward vector), `size` the box extents. Values read as finite numbers; `lip` limited to a sane range; a malformed `movedir` leaves the part closed (the safe default is the closed pose as before).
*   **`func_door_rotating`:** if it starts open, the pose is the angles turned by `distance` about the axis of the spawnflags (sign by the reverse bit). Where the turn cannot be expressed in the fixture's three angles unambiguously, the part is **not drawn** (see below).
*   **`func_movelinear`:** the origin plus `dir * startposition * movedistance` (clamped to 0..1 and a sane distance), as the water brush's placement does.
*   **The safe fallback: not drawn.** A part that starts open and whose open pose this function cannot place with confidence (a missing or malformed `movedir`, a rotation it cannot express) is **left out** of the picture: an open door usually stands in a wall, a floor or a frame, where it hides nothing, and drawing it closed is the known wrong. The part is counted in the coverage record (section 5) so the maintainer can see how many parts took that path.
*   **Hidden-at-start stays as it is** (`StartDisabled`, `rendermode 10`, RFC 33, 3, 9a); a part is dropped if it is hidden *or* starts open and cannot be placed.
*   **Where the part is stored.** The movable group (`movable` triangles, RFC 18, 4.4) keeps the part under its name (`movable-<model>`), now at the start pose. Nothing else about the group changes.
*   **Mesh version:** `MESH_VERSION` and `MESH_VERSION_GAME` each go up by one, so the stored maps are rebuilt once.

## 5. The census (so the effect is measured on all 28 maps)
RFC 32's coverage record gets a small `startStates` object per map: the counts of `func_door`, `func_door_rotating` and `func_movelinear` that start closed, that start open and were **placed at the open end**, and that start open and were **left out**; and the number of parts whose pose came from `startposition > 0`. The coverage page gets a short card "Where moving parts start" with the totals over the maps with a record and, per map, the numbers, ranked by parts that moved or were left out. After the deployment and the rebuild, the page shows which maps changed and by how much, as RFC 33's stage D does for the gaps. The `startStates` counts only count; they do not change the drawing.

## 6. Stages
*   **A (this RFC):** `startPose` for the three classes, the fallback, the census and its card, the mesh version, tests, the measurement on the three real maps below, the docs (RFC 18, 4.4 gets a pointer here; `docs/MAP_COVERAGE.md` a line).
*   **B (only if wanted):** the map's own I/O at the start (`logic_auto` `OnMapSpawn`, `ttt_logic_role OnPass`): a possible RFC of its own.

## 7. Tests (what must fail on a broken build)
*   `startPose` in plain code: a `func_door` with `spawnpos 0` or absent is not moved; `spawnpos 1` and `spawnflags & 1` move the origin by `|dir · size| - lip` along `movedir` for movedirs east, west, north, up and down and an oblique one; a negative `lip`; a malformed or missing `movedir` leaves it closed or (for an open-start part) not drawn per the rule; `func_movelinear` `startposition` 0, 0.5, 1 and out of range; a rotating door by `distance` about each axis and reversed; unknown values behave as the safe default.
*   The extractor on a small constructed BSP (RFC 18/33 build one): the pool-cover pattern (a slab of 1 202 units with `spawnpos 1`, `movedir 0 0 0`, `lip -8`) lands 1 210 units east of its origin; a part that starts open and is left out is absent from `movable` and counted; a `spawnpos 0` door is unchanged (a pinned-hash test: a map with no open-start parts gives the same bytes as before, apart from the version number the file carries).
*   Real-key spelling: the keyvalues are written by Hammer as `spawnpos`, `StartDisabled`, `movedir`, `lip`, `startposition` and the tests use that spelling and mixed case (the lesson of RFC 33, 9a).
*   The census: the counts on the constructed maps, the card's totals and ranking, an old record without `startStates` loads.
*   The mesh version numbers are bumped and still differ; a stored mesh of the old version is rebuilt.

## 8. Check on the real maps (required before it is called done)
Build and render `ttt_community_pool` (the pool cover must be gone from the pool: the water plane visible from above, from a free camera and low over the water; the bathroom doors "door 1" and "door 2" no longer filling their doorways), `ttt_67thway_rework_b1` (`trapdoor1`, `trapdoor2` and `scandoor` at their open ends or left out; nothing stray floating in the rooms) and `ttt_minecraft_b7_summer` (unchanged: no open-start doors), before and after, from the same cameras, and list for each moved or dropped part where it went and what the picture shows. A part drawn in a wrong place (a slab floating in a room, inside a floor where it shows) is a defect of this RFC and must be fixed or the part left out.

## 9. What cannot be verified here
*   Parts of the other 25 maps: they are measured by the census after the deployment, not here. A class or flag combination not seen on the three maps (a rotating door on another axis, a `movedir` in a parent's frame, `func_door` with a `parentname`: not drawn, as now) is covered by the tests and the safe fallback only.
*   That a door's open end lies where the formula says in every map (the engine's exact rule includes the model's own origin offset and the `lip` as "remaining size"); the three maps are the check.
*   The map's I/O at the start (section 3, last point).

## 10. As built (2026-11-17)
**What was built.** `startPose`, `startsOpen` (`backend/src/maps/bsp.ts`), wired into `extractMapEntities`; `startStates` in the coverage record (old records load without it); the card "Where moving parts start" on the coverage page; the registry feature `door start states` (and its row in `docs/MAP_COVERAGE.md`); `MESH_VERSION` 16 and `MESH_VERSION_GAME` 17. Tests: `rfc35_start_pose.test.ts`, `rfc35_page.test.ts`, and the pinned hashes of `coverage.test.ts` and `rfc33_elements.test.ts` moved to the new generation (with the numbers put back, the old hashes come out again: no byte of a map without an open-start part changed).

**Differences from the design.**
*   The travel of a `func_door` is `|dx * sizeX| + |dy * sizeY| + |dz * sizeZ| - lip` (the sum over the axes, as the engine does), which is the design's `|dir . size| - lip` for every axis-aligned `movedir`. The engine also subtracts 2 from each size (`OBBSize - 2`); this build follows the RFC and does not, so a door is placed 2 units farther than in the game (pool cover: 1 210, the engine 1 208).
*   A rotating door needs neither `movedir` nor a box: Source adds `distance` (90 by default) to the entity's angles component by component (X axis = roll, Y = pitch, Z = yaw, bit 2 reverses), and the faces are drawn through the same angle matrix, so every axis is exact and none is left out for that reason. It is left out only for a `distance` that is not a sane number.
*   Left out (not drawn, counted): a door that starts open with no `movedir` or one that is not three numbers, no model box (models lump unreadable or the model not in it), a `lip` or travel outside 0 to 16 384 (a lip larger than the size), or a linear mover with `startposition` above 0 and no usable `movedir`. The models lump is read only when the entity lump names a `func_door`.
*   `spawnpos`, when it is a number, rules; `spawnflags` bit 1 only counts when there is none. Hidden (RFC 33) and parented parts are decided before and are not counted in `startStates`.
*   The origin is rounded to a millionth of a unit (the cosine of 90 degrees is 6e-17).
