# RFC 30: A Spacing Scale and Layout Primitives for the Stylesheet

> **Status: built (2026-11-12), stages A, B and C, measured in Chromium (section 7); the ratchets are at 31 allow-listed selectors and 0 raw inline values.** Asked for by the maintainer after the voice page's cards were spaced differently from the maps page's ("why is this so inconsistent? aren't we writing and using our own design system?"). The [UI design notes](../UI_DESIGN.md) now have a spacing section; before this the notes described the colours, the cards and a few margin helpers, but nothing about spacing, and the stylesheet had no named scale for it.

## 1. Abstract
The stylesheet (`backend/src/components/styles.ts`) has a palette of named colours (`--ink`, `--paper`, ...) with a rule for using it, a card (`.f`, `.pn`) and a handful of helpers. Space between things has no such system. Measured on 2026-11-12: the `gap` property alone takes fourteen different values, the margins and paddings about twenty-eight in all (from `.05rem` to `2rem`), and every page that lays out cards writes its own numbers (`.g` and `.pair` use `1.2rem .8rem`, `.replay` `1.2rem`, `.nums` `1.1rem .8rem`, the voice page first used `1rem`). Nothing says which number a gap between cards is, so a new page guesses, and the page looks different. This RFC names a small scale of steps, gives the layout of cards one shared rule, moves the existing rules onto the scale, and adds a test that stops new raw numbers from creeping back.

## 2. What is there and what is missing
*   **There:** colours as variables on `:root`, named by use, with the rule "use a variable, add a new one only when a colour is used in three places" (UI design notes, section 2b); the card and its tab colour; the margin helpers `mt1`, `mt6`, `mt12`, `m0`, `ml6`, `mb6`.
*   **Missing:** a scale for space; a shared rule for "a column of cards" and "a grid of cards"; a place in the notes that says how far apart things go.
*   **The incident.** Every card has a coloured tab `0.7rem` high above it. The tab sticks out into the space above the card, so the gap between two cards is the gap minus the tab. On the maps page that leaves about `0.5rem` of clear space below the card above; on the first version of the voice page, with `1rem` written by hand, about `0.3rem`, and the grey tab nearly touched the card above. Nothing in the code told whoever wrote the voice page that the number existed.

## 3. The scale
Named by use, like the colours. One `rem` is the page's root size.

| Variable | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--space-1` | `.25rem` | the least: between a label and its field, inside a control's rows |
| `--space-2` | `.5rem` | between items of a row, between a heading and its text |
| `--space-3` | `.8rem` | between blocks of text, between the columns of a grid of cards |
| `--space-4` | `1.2rem` | **between cards** (rows of a grid, items of a column), between the parts of a page |
| `--space-5` | `2rem` | between the big parts of a page, the room around an empty state |
| `--tab` | `.7rem` | the height of a card's tab (`.f:before`, `.pn:before`); a stack or grid of cards leaves `--tab` and a little more above its first row |

*   **Every existing value goes to the nearest step; a tie is decided by the role** (a `1rem` between controls inside a card is `--space-3`, a `1rem` between cards is `--space-4`). The agent doing it writes the mapping down in the RFC's section 7 as it goes.
*   **Details inside a control are not layout** and stay as they are: the `.05rem` to `.2rem` paddings of buttons and pills, a one-pixel border, a `-1px` margin that overlaps a border. They are listed in the test's allow-list (section 5) by selector, so the list can only get shorter.
*   **No numbers in the notes' vocabulary that the stylesheet does not have:** the notes list the six variables and their uses, and say that a new step is added only when a space is used in three places, as for colours.

## 4. Layout primitives
*   **A column of cards: `.stack`** (flex, column, `gap: var(--space-4)`, `padding-top: calc(var(--tab) + var(--space-2))`: clear space for the first card's tab). The voice page's `.vcs` becomes `.stack`.
*   **A grid of cards: `.g`** (the existing class, `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(13rem, 1fr))`, `gap: var(--space-4) var(--space-3)`, the same top clearance) and **`.pair`** and the other card grids use the same two numbers; their column minimums stay what they are.
*   **Text blocks inside a card** keep the card's own padding; the space between the blocks is `--space-3`, and between a heading and its text `--space-2`.
*   The page's own `h1` and the paragraph under it keep their margins; what follows them is a stack or grid, which brings its own top clearance, so a page needs no extra `margin-top` before its first card.

## 5. A test that keeps it so
`backend/test/spacing.test.ts` reads the stylesheet text (`PUBLIC_CSS` and `ADMIN_CSS`) and the inline `style="..."` of the page components:
*   every value of `gap`, `margin*` and `padding*` in a **layout rule** is `0`, a `var(--space-N)` or `var(--tab)` (or a `calc` of those), unless the selector is on a written allow-list of controls and details with a reason beside each;
*   the allow-list and the count of raw values in inline styles are a **ratchet**: the test holds the numbers they have now and fails when they grow (an agent or a person lowers the constant when they shrink it);
*   the six variables exist on `:root` and the notes name each of them;
*   the card tab height in `.f:before` is `var(--tab)`.
The test checks text, not looks. What it cannot do is say a page looks right; section 6 does.

## 6. Checking that nothing breaks
The change touches nearly every page, so it is measured, not judged by eye: before and after the change, in real Chromium at 1100 px and 390 px wide, for each page the tests can render (home, servers, a server's page, roles, a role, stats and its tabs, a round, a player, maps and a map, the replay page, settings, the voice page, the servers admin page), the page's total height and the box (position and size) of every card, heading and form field are recorded; the report lists every page's height change and every box that moved by more than 4 px, with a screenshot pair of anything that moved more than 12 px. A change that is the point of the RFC (two grids that used to differ now agree) is called that. The maintainer looks at the screenshot pairs before the change is merged.

## 7. Stages, what was built and what was measured
*   **A (built):** the six variables on `:root`; `.stack` (public stylesheet); the shared rule `.g,.pair,.nums` (gaps `--space-4 --space-3`, `padding-top: calc(var(--tab) + var(--space-2))`; only the column minimums are their own; `.tiles` of the admin pages has the same numbers); the voice page's `.vcs` is `.stack`; the `.f:before` tab is `var(--tab)` high and `calc(-1 * var(--tab))` up; the section "Spacing" of the notes; `backend/test/spacing.test.ts` with the two ratchets.
*   **B (built):** every `gap`, `margin*` and layout `padding*` of `PUBLIC_CSS` and `ADMIN_CSS` is `0`, `auto`, a step or a `calc` of steps, except 31 selectors on the allow-list in the test, each with a reason.
*   **C (built):** the inline `style="margin..."`, `padding` and `gap` of the components (29 of them, and the `gap` of an `x-bind:style`) are steps or helper classes; none is raw (ratchet 0).
*   **Not planned:** a type scale (font sizes) or a scale for widths; if the same kind of drift shows up there, a new RFC.

### 7.1 The mapping
Nearest step wins (`0` counts as a step, so `.1rem` and below go to `0` where it is layout); a tie is decided by the role.

| Old value | Step | Where, and why where it was a tie |
|---|---|---|
| `.1rem` | `0` | `h1` margin, `.hint` margin, the vertical padding of `.jump` (1.6 px) |
| `.2rem`, `.25rem`, `.3rem`, `.35rem` | `--space-1` | label to field, caption above/below, `.f .ft` padding, `.crumbs`, `.sections` top, `.list`, `.warn`, `.scale`, `.split`, `.dots` rows, `.filt .n`, `.pill` margin, the `.pn p` margin, `.call h3`, the `.vc .cap` and `.vc .person` paddings (`.35rem` is `.1` from `.25` and `.15` from `.5`) |
| `.4rem`, `.45rem`, `.5rem`, `.6rem` | `--space-2` | items of a row, a heading and its text, `.bar`, `form.find`, `.actions`, `.tools`, `.filt .chips`, `.toolbar label`, `.src`, `.dot`, `.scale`; `.6rem` is `.1` from `.5` and `.2` from `.8` |
| `.7rem`, `.75rem`, `.8rem`, `.9rem` | `--space-3` | the padding of a card (`.f` was `.7 .9 .75`, now `.8` on all sides; `.pn` `.9 1.1 1` is now `.8 1.2`), `.foot`, `.filt`, `.actions` top, `.fld`, `.cfg form`, `.copy`, `details`, `form.inline` top, `.sections` bottom, `.tile` gap |
| `1rem` (tie of `.8` and `1.2`) | `--space-3` | between controls and blocks inside a card or a row: `.vc` and its rows, `.topbar`, `.head`, `.plans` gap, `.toolbar` padding, `.call`, `.fld.row`, `.toast`, `.view [data-status]`, the inline `margin:1rem 0 0` of the map and stats pages, the `mt1` of a `.foot` and of a link under a card |
| `1rem` (the same tie) | `--space-4` | between cards and parts of a page: the margin above `.f.big` and `.pn`, `.plans` margin-top, the side padding of `.replay.theater`, the old `mt1` on a card (now `mt4`, which `mt12` was too) |
| `1.1rem`, `1.2rem`, `1.3rem`, `1.4rem`, `1.5rem` | `--space-4` | rows of a grid (`.g`, `.pair`, `.nums`, `.tiles`), the stack, `.replay` and its aside, the big card's padding, `h2` top, `h2+.f`, `.tools` and `.toolbar` column gap, `.cfg`, `.jump`, `.lay`, `.pn h3` |
| `2rem` | `--space-5` | the admin `.stack`, now `.parts` (the big parts of a page), the empty state of the role list |
| `.7rem` (`.tiles` and `.f:before`) | `--tab` | the height of a tab; the top padding of `.tiles` was `.7rem` (no room for the tab), now `--tab + --space-2` like every grid |
| `.9rem` top padding of `.nums`/`.pair`, `1rem` of `.g` | `calc(var(--tab) + var(--space-2))` = `1.2rem` | the room for the first row's tab, one number for every grid and stack |

Helpers: `mt1` (`1rem`), `mt6`, `mt12`, `ml6`, `mb6` are gone, replaced by one per step: `mt1` to `mt4` (`--space-1` to `--space-4`, top only), `sp1` to `sp3` (the space above, the other margins 0), `ml2`, `mb2`, `m0`. Old uses: `mt1` on a card and `mt12` became `mt4`; `mt1` on a `.foot`, the home page's `h1` and a link became `mt3`; `mt6` became `mt2`; `ml6` and the inline `margin-left:.5rem` became `ml2`; `mb6` became `mb2`. The inline margins used three or more times became `sp1` (`.2rem 0 0`), `sp2` (`.4`, `.5` and `.6rem 0 0`) and `sp3` (`.8` and `1rem 0 0`); the voice page's three `margin-top:.35rem` are `mt1`.

### 7.2 What stays raw (the allow-list)
31 selectors, each with a reason in `backend/test/spacing.test.ts`; the test also fails when an entry holds no raw value any more, so the list only shrinks.
*   **Details inside a control** (the padding is the control's size): `button`, `input[type=search]`, `.sections a`, `.bar a`, `.filt a`, `.stamp`, `.res`, `.tape`, `.btn`, `.pill`, `.tog`, `.vc .tog`, `.f input...`, `.mark`, `select / .dk`, `.ab`, `.team`, `.status`, `.side .find input`, `.jump a`, and `.lvl b` (the `-1px` overlap margin).
*   **Dense rows, where a step would add or take 1 to 3 px from every row** (a list of rounds has dozens): `.ledger`, `.ledger div`, `table.set th|td`, `table.log th|td`, `.win`, `.prow`, `.ritem`.
*   **Reserved room, not space between things:** `.cfg` (`padding-bottom:6.5rem` under the fixed save bar).
The RFC text of section 3 named only buttons and pills; the rows of tables and lists are the same kind of detail (the row height is set by the padding), and are on the list for that reason, not because they were hard.

What changed, by cause (all of it is a change of one of the numbers above; nothing else moved):
*   **Intended: two things that used to differ now agree.** The grids of cards leave the same room above the first row and the same gaps (`.g` was `1rem` above, `.nums` and `.pair` `.9rem`, `.tiles` `.7rem` and `1.5rem` between rows, `.replay` `1.2rem`: now all `1.2rem` above the first tab and `1.2rem .8rem` between), so the stats and player pages grow by 5 px above each grid (+3.0% at 1100, 21 px; this is the largest height change, and it is a change of the two grids that used to be tighter than the maps page) and the server page's tiles by 8 px. The voice page, the reference, loses 30 to 36 px (-2.3% at 1100, -1.5% at 390): its controls inside a card were `1rem` apart and are `--space-3` (`.8rem`) like the controls of every other card; the gap between its cards (`.stack`: `1.2rem`) and the room above the first (now `1.2rem` instead of `1rem`) are the grids'.
*   **Intended: the padding of a card.** `.f` was `.7rem .9rem .75rem` and is `.8rem` on every side: each card grows by 2.4 px in height and is 3.2 px wider inside (the role list: 8 cards, +12 px, +1.6% at 1100; at 390 +16 px). `.f.big` (`1.1rem 1.3rem 1.3rem`) and `.pn` (`.9rem 1.1rem 1rem`) are on the scale as well: a box of the pages of one round or one role moves 5 to 7 px.
*   **Intended: admin.** `.lay` and `.tiles` gaps `1.5rem` are `1.2rem` (the side panel is 4.8 px nearer on a phone, and the first `h1` moves up 10 to 14 px there together with it); the settings fields `.7rem .9rem` are `.8rem`.
*   **Not intended and not fixable on the scale: a text wraps differently.** The page gutter `.w` was `1rem`, a tie; both neighbours move the content width by 6.4 px (`.8rem` made the roles list fit a fourth column at 1100: -18% in height; at 390 it changed which tab strips wrap and moved the voice page by -7% and the settings page by -2.4%). `--space-4` was chosen (the room around the whole page is a part of a page, section 3) because it leaves every 1100 page's columns alone. Its one visible cost is the view log at 390 (+6.2%, +34 px): the paragraph under its `h1` is one line longer. A gutter of `calc(2 * var(--space-2))` would keep every width as it was; it is the same number written as a calc, and it was not used because the scale says nearest step. **Decided 2026-11-12, on the reviewer's measurements (the three options on real pages): the gutter stays `1rem` as its own variable `--gutter`, documented as an exception and not a step.** With `--space-4` the view log's paragraph gained a line at 390 px (+6.2 %); with `--space-3` the roles list fitted a fourth column at 1100 px only by an exact float tie (4 x 13rem + 3 x .8rem = 54.4rem, a fragile design) and nine pages reflowed; with `--gutter` no page moved by more than 0.7 %. The gutter is the frame of the page, not a space between things, so a step was never the honest name for it.
*   **Page heights at 1100 stay within 3%** (largest +3.0% on two pages, -2.3% on the voice page, intended); at 390 within 2.3% except the view log's one line.
*   **Not measured:** the pages that need a player's session or a game state the seed has no way to make (the voice page after "Join voice": the people list, the meter and the notes filled in; the replay page after the track is played and the 3D view is loaded; the role page for roles with add-ons; a failed map scan; the admin `/servers` page with a token shown); hover, focus and pressed states (the existing layout tests that measured those still pass, see below); the in-game browser (Chrome 86) itself, which cannot be run here: the CSS added uses only custom properties, `calc()`, flex and grid gaps and `min()` in a rule that was already there.

Screenshot pairs of the pages where a box moved by more than 12 px (the harness's `before-` and `after-` files) were kept next to the measurements; the maintainer looks at them before the merge.

### 7.4 What the RFC got wrong or left out
*   **The name `.stack` was taken.** The admin stylesheet already had a `.stack` (a column with `2rem` between the group sections of a server's roles). The new `.stack` is the column of cards; the old one is `.parts`, with `--space-5`.
*   **The allow-list is longer than section 3 suggested** (31, not "buttons and pills"): see 7.2.
*   **There is no step below `.25rem`,** so `.1rem` to `.12rem` of layout becomes `0` (a 1.6 px change each); the row paddings of tables and lists stay raw instead.
*   **`.pair`, `.nums` and `.tiles` were not "the same two numbers" before** (`.nums` was `1.1rem .8rem`, `.tiles` `1.5rem 1rem`); making them so is what moves the stats and server pages.
*   **The gutter** was decided (7.3): `--gutter`, `1rem`, not a step.
