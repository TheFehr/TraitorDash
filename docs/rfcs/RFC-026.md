# RFC 26: Record Everything That Happens in a Round

> **Status: stage A built (2026-11-06): chat (opt-in), shop purchases and bodies found.** Not built yet: health, and the rest of section 4. Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-06 ("what else to record: everything"), answering the open question of [RFC 12](./RFC-012.md), section 9.

## 1. Abstract
A replay (RFC 12) knew where players were, when they died, what they were revived by (RFC 14), their role changes and the doors they used (RFC 20). It did not know what they said, bought, found or how hurt they were. This RFC adds those as events in the same track, step by step, until the replay can tell the round's whole story.

## 2. Design
*   **One list of events** (`events` in the track, RFC 14, 4.3): `{ t, p, k, ... }` with `t` in milliseconds since the round began and `p` a player of the round. Each kind has a cap of its own in the bridge (a round that is chatty cannot use up the room of the others); a kind that reaches its cap marks the track truncated. The backend takes at most 3 840 events and checks every field of every kind; an unknown kind refuses the whole track.
*   **Nothing blocks the game.** The hooks return nothing, run under `pcall`, and do one lookup when nothing is being recorded.
*   **Deploy the backend first.** A backend from before this RFC refuses a track with an event kind it does not know, so a new bridge against an old backend loses its tracks (with a log line). The other way round is fine.

## 3. Stage A: chat, purchases, bodies (built)
| Kind | Fields | Hook | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `say` | `text` (at most 200 characters, control characters made spaces, invalid UTF-8 reduced to ASCII), `ch`: `all`, `team` (the team's own chat, the traitors' among them) or `dead`. **Only with `traitordash_track_chat` 1**, read when the round begins | `PlayerSay` | 1 024 |
| `buy` | `item` (the class), `it` true for a passive item | `TTT2OrderedEquipment`, `TTTOrderedEquipment` (the same purchase within a second is one) | 512 |
| `found` | `of`: whose body, when that player was in the round | `TTTBodyFound` | 256 |

Only players of the round are recorded (a spectator who was not in it is not). The replay shows them in a feed ("Chat, shop and bodies", every line a link that seeks to it) and in the selected player's own list. They are not marks on the time bar.

**Tested** with the bridge in the Fengari harness (the hooks as the game calls them) and the backend's checks. **Not yet seen on the real server:** the names and arguments of the three hooks are those of TTT and TTT2 as the maintainer of this code knows them; whether `TTT2OrderedEquipment` and `TTTBodyFound` fire as assumed is for the first real round to show (a round with a purchase and a body found).

## 4. Next stages (not built)
*   **B, health:** a health value with each position sample (delta coded, mostly zeros) and the damage events (who hurt whom, with what, how much), so the replay can draw a health bar and tell who was hurt by whom before a death.
*   **C, the rest:** weapon pickups and drops and the weapon in hand; shots fired; credits gained and spent; C4 planted, defused and exploded; DNA scans; traitor-button use; karma changes; who a spectator watches; voice chat (who talks, not what); the round's phase changes. Each needs its hook checked on the real server first; the stage is as long as that list.

## 5. Privacy
Chat is the first thing in a track that is what a person *wrote*, not what the game did, so it is **off by default** (decided 2026-11-06): the ConVar `traitordash_track_chat` (a bool, also in the dashboard's settings of the bridge, RFC 23, group "Position tracking", applied from the next round) turns it on. With it on, a replay is for signed-in users only (RFC 13), but every signed-in user sees all of it, **including a team's private chat and the dead's chat**, after the round. That is the point of a replay and is also a spoiler and an exposure; the operator who turns it on should tell the players (the server's message of the day, for example).

## 6. Open
*   Whether the feed should be filterable by kind or player; it is not.
